Purges only the cached pages that showed a term when that term is edited. A term archive and its feed, an entry page that shows the term as a chip, and a page that embeds it through a term field are now stored under that term's own cache tag, and renaming the term or saving only its meta purges it. A term edit no longer purges every page of the entry types its taxonomy lists.
